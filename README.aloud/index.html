Your files. Not a bookstore.

README.aloud works with compatible local documents and ebooks. It extracts text and sends it to playback.

EPUB MOBI PRC PDF (text layer) TXT RTF
Protected files are rejected. Scanned PDFs are not sent to OCR.
✓No DRM circumvention.
✓No cloud conversion.
✓No audiobook export.

Private by design.

Your documents stay on your device. The v1 product has no account, backend, document upload, analytics/tracking SDK, advertising, cloud AI, or subscription.

No account

Open the app and use it. There is no sign-in flow.

No upload

Your document is processed locally rather than sent to a service.

No tracking

No third-party analytics or advertising layer.

No paywall

The app is fully functional. Optional tips do not unlock features.


A simple lightweight text player. Nothing more complicated than that.

README.aloud is built around a simple idea: your files already contain the text. Your device already has a voice. The app provides the player.
