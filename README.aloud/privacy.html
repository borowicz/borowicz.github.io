Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Sheep Me to Sleep (“the app”) is a small app for counting jumping sheep. It is made by Andrzej Borowicz.

Data we do not collect

The app does not require an account and does not include a cloud backend.

  • No name, email, or user profile
  • No analytics or advertising trackers
  • No sleep, health, or wellness data
  • No location

Settings such as sheep mode, tempo, sounds, and the session timer stay on your device.

Tips

Optional tips are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. We do not receive your full payment card details. Tips do not unlock features and do not create a “supporter” account.

Children

The app is a simple animation utility and is not directed at collecting data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change. The current version will always be at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions use the support page: https://borowicz.github.io/sheep/
