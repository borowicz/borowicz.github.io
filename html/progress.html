Year Me Up · Life Progress

Your life, in numbers.

The primary layer is calculated from time and astronomy. Population-based numbers are deliberately separated into Estimated / Fun Stats.

Life Progress is a mathematical model, not a prediction of an individual lifespan. Change the target to explore a different horizon.
