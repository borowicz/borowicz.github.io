Privacy Policy — Year Me Up

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Year Me Up (“the App”) is a widget-first app for macOS, iPhone, and iPad. It does not require an account, use advertising or tracking, or send your personal data to a server operated by the developer. Some optional features send the information needed to fulfil your request directly to Apple services, as described below.

This policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used and stored, when information is sent to Apple, and the choices available to you.

Data processed and stored locally

The App processes the following information locally and stores the data it needs in its app container or the shared App Group used by the App and its widgets. The App does not send this information to a server operated by the developer. Standard operating-system backup settings may apply separately:

  • Calendar events — read only after you turn on Calendar integration and explicitly allow Calendar access. Used to prepare widget snapshots on your device. Events are not uploaded and are not used for tracking.
  • Contact names and birthdays — read only after you allow Contacts access and turn on contact birthdays. Used only for widgets. Contacts are not uploaded and are not used for tracking.
  • Custom photos — imported only when you select them with the system photo picker or choose a file. The App re-encodes the image pixels rather than retaining the original metadata, then stores the prepared image and widget-specific crops in the App Group. You can rename, crop, and delete imported backgrounds. The App does not request unrestricted photo-library access.
  • Custom date events — rows imported from a CSV file that you select are stored locally in the App Group and may be shown in supported widgets. You can export your custom list, export active events, or clear the custom list from Dates & Events settings. Exported files are saved to a location you choose; any copy you share or save outside the App is no longer controlled by the App.
  • Settings, widget configurations, and prepared data — stored locally and in the App Group. Widgets read prepared snapshots only; they do not independently request Calendar, Contacts, or Location permission.

Local settings, imported events, and photos are retained while the relevant app data remains on your device, including after the trial ends. You can delete custom photos individually and clear your custom event list in the App. If you uninstall the App, the operating system normally removes its app-specific data, but device backups and copies you exported or shared may persist according to the services and settings you use. The App has no developer-operated account or server from which to retrieve or delete a personal-data profile.

Location

Location is used only after you turn on “current location” and explicitly allow Location access.

  • Sunrise and sunset times are calculated on your device.
  • When you enable current location, the App may send your location to Apple WeatherKit to request local weather. This is a request to Apple, not to a Year Me Up server.
  • Manual place search sends the place or address query you enter to Apple’s geocoding service. It does not require Location Services permission.

Location is not used for tracking, advertising, or profiling. The App does not request Always-location permission.

Apple services

The App uses the following Apple services:

  • StoreKit — for the optional 14-day trial (free non-consumable) and one-time lifetime unlock. Purchases, restores, and Family Sharing are handled by Apple. There is no subscription and no automatic charge.
  • WeatherKit — when you enable current location, as described above.
  • Apple geocoding — for manual place search.
  • App Store and operating-system services — Apple's services process app distribution and standard system functions, including network requests initiated by the optional features you use.

Apple’s own privacy policies apply to data processed by these services.

What we do not do

  • No user account or sign-in.
  • No tracking, analytics SDKs, or advertising identifiers.
  • No sale of personal data.
  • No developer-operated backend that receives your calendar, contacts, photos, or location.
  • No camera, microphone, or unrestricted photo-library access.

Widgets and Live Activity

Widgets and the Countdown Live Activity (iOS) read only data the App has already prepared and published to the App Group. They do not independently request system permissions.

Children

Year Me Up does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It is a general productivity app.

Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the App or applicable requirements. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates the latest revision.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the support channel listed on the App Store product page or at the support address provided in App Store Connect.
