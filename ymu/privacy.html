Privacy Policy — Year Me Up

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Year Me Up (“the App”) is a widget-first app for macOS, iPhone, and iPad. It does not require an account. It does not track you, does not sell your data, and does not send your personal data to a developer-operated server.

This policy describes what data the App can access, where that data stays, and when any data is sent to Apple.

Data that stays on your device

The following data is read or stored only on your device (and in the shared App Group used by the App and its widgets). It is not uploaded to a developer server:

  • Calendar events — read only after you turn on Calendar integration and explicitly allow Calendar access. Used to prepare widget snapshots on your device. Events are not uploaded and are not used for tracking.
  • Contact names and birthdays — read only after you allow Contacts access and turn on contact birthdays. Used only for widgets. Contacts are not uploaded and are not used for tracking.
  • Custom photos — imported only when you select them with the system Photos picker or a file you choose. Images are re-encoded without GPS or EXIF metadata and stored in the App Group so widgets can display them. The App does not request full photo-library access.
  • Settings, widget configurations, and other app data — stored locally and in the App Group. Widgets read prepared snapshots only; they never request Calendar, Contacts, or Location permission themselves.

All of the above remains available to you after a trial ends or if you delete the App (subject to normal iOS/macOS behavior). You can manage or remove retained photos and settings inside the App.

Location

Location is used only after you turn on “current location” and explicitly allow Location access.

  • Sunrise and sunset times are calculated on your device.
  • When you enable current location, the App may send your location to Apple WeatherKit to request local weather. This is a request to Apple, not to a Year Me Up server.
  • Manual place search uses Apple’s geocoding service and does not require Location Services permission.

Location is not used for tracking, advertising, or profiling. The App does not request Always-location permission.

Apple services

The App uses the following Apple services:

  • StoreKit — for the optional 14-day trial (free non-consumable) and one-time lifetime unlock. Purchases, restores, and Family Sharing are handled by Apple. There is no subscription and no automatic charge.
  • WeatherKit — when you enable current location, as described above.
  • Apple geocoding — for manual place search.
  • App Store / system services — standard Apple infrastructure (HTTPS).

Apple’s own privacy policies apply to data processed by these services.

What we do not do

  • No user account or sign-in.
  • No tracking, analytics SDKs, or advertising identifiers.
  • No sale of personal data.
  • No developer-operated backend that receives your calendar, contacts, photos, or location.
  • No camera, microphone, or unrestricted photo-library access.

Widgets and Live Activity

Widgets and the Countdown Live Activity (iOS) read only data the App has already prepared and published to the App Group. They do not independently request system permissions.

Children

Year Me Up does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It is a general productivity app.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when it does. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the support channel listed on the App Store product page or at the support address provided in App Store Connect.
