Privacy Policy — Year Me Up

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Year Me Up (“the App”) is a native app for macOS, iPhone, and iPad. It does not require an account and does not operate a developer server for collecting your personal data. The App does not use advertising or third-party analytics, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not sell personal data.

This policy explains what information the App accesses, how the App stores it, and when information may be sent to Apple services. The App does not provide its own cloud synchronisation service.

Information stored on your device

The App stores its settings, widget configurations, prepared widget data, and user-created content locally, including in the shared App Group used by the App and its widgets. The App does not send the following content to a developer-operated server:

  • Calendar events — read only when you enable calendar integration and grant Calendar permission. They are used to prepare calendar-related information on your device.
  • Contact names and birthdays — read only when you grant Contacts permission and enable contact birthdays. They are used to prepare relevant information on your device.
  • Custom dates and events — events you create or import from a CSV file are stored locally in the App Group for use by the App and supported widgets. You can export your custom event list to CSV or clear that list in Dates & Events settings. The App does not upload the CSV or event list.
  • Custom photos and backgrounds — images are imported only when you explicitly select them through the system photo picker or a file-selection interface. The App processes selected images for use as backgrounds and re-encodes them without carrying over the source image’s EXIF/GPS metadata. The resulting images are stored locally in the App Group and can be removed through the relevant photo/background controls.
  • Settings and widget snapshots — preferences, widget configuration, and prepared data are stored locally so the App and widgets can display your chosen content.

Retention and deletion

Expiry of the 14-day trial does not, by itself, mean that your locally stored settings, custom events, or photos are deleted. You can remove custom events and custom photos using the controls provided by the App. Exported CSV files are saved to a destination you choose; if you later share or save a file outside the App, that copy is subject to the destination service or recipient’s practices.

Deleting the App may remove locally stored App data. Do not rely on uninstalling the App as a way to preserve or export your information. Export any custom events you want to keep before uninstalling. If you have enabled device backups, operating-system backup settings and Apple’s handling of backups also apply.

Location, place search, and weather

  • On-device calculations — sunrise, sunset, and related astronomical values are calculated on your device using the selected location.
  • Current location — when you choose to use the device’s current location and grant Location permission, the App may use that location for location-dependent features.
  • Manual place search — searching for a place uses Apple’s geocoding service. This does not require Location Services permission, but your search input is sent to Apple to resolve the place.
  • Weather — when the App requests weather from Apple WeatherKit, it sends the coordinates of the selected location to Apple. Those coordinates may come from your current device location or from a place you selected manually. This request goes to Apple, not to a Year Me Up server.

The App does not use location for advertising, tracking, or profiling, and it does not request Always-location permission. Apple’s processing of geocoding and weather requests is governed by Apple’s terms and privacy practices.

Purchases and Apple services

The App uses Apple services for the optional 14-day full-access trial and the one-time lifetime unlock. StoreKit handles purchase processing, purchase restoration, and Family Sharing where supported. Year Me Up does not receive your payment-card details. The App has no subscription or automatic-renewal charge.

Weather requests and manual place search may also use Apple WeatherKit and Apple’s geocoding service, as described above. Information processed by these services is subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy and the terms applicable to each service.

Widgets and Live Activity

Widgets and the Countdown Live Activity on iOS read data already prepared by the App and published to the shared App Group. They do not independently request Calendar, Contacts, or Location permission and do not send widget content to a developer-operated server.

What the App does not do

  • No account or sign-in requirement.
  • No advertising, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No sale of personal data or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • No developer-operated backend receiving your calendar, contacts, custom photos, custom events, or location.
  • No unrestricted photo-library access, camera access, or microphone access is required for the features described in this policy.

Children

Year Me Up is a general date, time, and widget app. The developer does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the App or a developer-operated service.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as the App changes. The revision date at the top identifies the latest version published at this address.
