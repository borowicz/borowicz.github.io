Sheep Me
to Sleep

Sheep jump. You count. Completely free.

A tiny native Apple app that does one absurdly simple thing: sheep jump across the screen, and you count them.

Pick a single sheep or a flock, set the pace, and turn the counter on or off. Optional audio includes ambient sound, spoken counting, and hoofbeats. A session timer can run for 15, 30, 60, or 90 minutes, or stay on until you stop it.

You can send the picture to a TV with AirPlay. There is a Watch companion too.

The joke, on every screen.

Same sheep. Different screens.

One flock. Many screens.

One simple app across the Apple ecosystem.
