LastOpened for macOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

LastOpened is designed to work locally on your Mac. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data collection

LastOpened does not collect personal data. The app does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, trackers, user accounts, or a developer-operated backend.

Local data

Application history, file metadata, searches, settings, and optional Safari bookmarks are stored locally on the user's Mac.

LastOpened accesses files only inside folders explicitly selected by the user. Folder access is read-only and uses macOS security-scoped bookmarks.

Optional Safari access

Safari access is optional. It is used only after the user enables the feature and grants the required macOS permission. LastOpened saves pages only for services or domains explicitly selected by the user.

Safari page titles and URLs are stored locally on the Mac and are not sent to the developer.

In-App Purchases

Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. LastOpened does not receive or store payment card details or other payment information.

Diagnostics

LastOpened does not include a developer-operated telemetry or crash reporting service. Apple may process diagnostics according to the user's macOS privacy settings and Apple's own privacy policy.

Data deletion

Locally stored application history and bookmarks can be deleted from the app's settings. Removing the app also removes its locally stored application data, subject to normal macOS behavior.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when LastOpened changes. The current version will remain available on the app's support website.
