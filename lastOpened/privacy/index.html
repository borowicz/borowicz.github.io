LastOpened for macOS · Version 1.1

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

LastOpened is a local-first macOS utility. Application history, selected-folder metadata, bookmarks and optional source results are processed on your Mac. The app does not send this information to the developer or operate a data-collection server.

Data collection and network use

LastOpened has no user-account system, analytics or advertising SDK, first-party telemetry, or developer-operated backend. Its current Privacy Manifest declares no tracking and no collected data. The app does not upload application history, file names, searches, settings, selected URLs, Calendar or Reminders metadata, Photos or Music metadata, or Terminal commands to the developer.

Opening a website, file or another application may cause Safari, the destination application, macOS or Apple services to access a network. This is not a telemetry connection to a LastOpened developer server.

Application activity

LastOpened observes macOS workspace notifications for application launch, activation and termination. A local application record may contain the app name, bundle identifier, local bundle path, latest activity timestamps and an optional cumulative launch counter. The app keeps one bounded latest-state record per application, not a complete event timeline.

Up to 250 application records are retained. The available retention choices are 7 days, 30 days (the default for new installations), 90 days, or until you delete the data. Records are pruned locally when they expire or exceed the record limit. You can pause or disable history, clear it, change retention, or ignore selected apps.

LastOpened does not measure foreground time, session duration, idle time or productivity. It does not collect general window titles, typed text, clipboard contents or screenshots.

Files and selected folders

File discovery starts only after you select folders through the standard macOS picker. Access is read-only and restored with app-scoped security bookmarks. Search and filtering use file metadata within the folders you authorized; the app does not read document contents to index or filter them. A bounded fallback metadata scan checks no more than 5,000 entries per run.

LastOpened does not request Full Disk Access and does not scan your entire home folder or disk by default. Quick Look previews are shown only when you explicitly request a preview through the macOS interface.

Optional Calendar integration

Calendar is off until you enable it in Settings. LastOpened then requests the macOS Calendar permission needed to read local event metadata. macOS labels this permission Full Access because EventKit does not provide a read-only Calendar permission. LastOpened uses the access to display event metadata; it does not create, edit or delete Calendar data. Opening an event asks Calendar to show its date and does not change the event.

Optional Reminders integration

Reminders is off until you enable it in Settings. LastOpened requests Reminders Full Access to read local reminder metadata. It displays at most 100 incomplete reminders and does not create, edit or delete reminders or keep a separate Reminders activity history. Opening a reminder launches the Reminders app; it does not select or modify the specific reminder.

Optional Photos integration

Photos is off until you enable it in Settings. PhotoKit and macOS may describe the permission as Read & Write Access, but LastOpened uses it to read metadata for up to 100 recent, non-hidden assets available within your current full or limited Photos access. It can display small on-device thumbnails; thumbnail requests do not download assets from iCloud. The app does not create, edit or delete Photos assets or retain a Photos activity history.

Opening an asset normally opens Photos. A separate optional direct-open action may request Apple Automation permission to spotlight the selected local asset. If Automation is unavailable or denied, Photos opens normally.

Optional Music integration

Music is off until you enable it in Settings. After you grant Media & Apple Music access, LastOpened reads local Music library metadata for up to 100 songs with a last-played date. It does not read audio contents, start playback, modify the library, persist a Music activity history or send Music data to the developer.

Optional Terminal history

Terminal history is off until you enable it and explicitly select a local .zsh_history file through the macOS picker. Access is read-only and restored with an app-scoped security bookmark. The viewer displays up to 200 recent distinct commands in memory and offers Copy Command. LastOpened does not execute or rerun commands and does not create a separate command-history database.

Optional Safari bookmarks

Safari URL bookmarking is off by default, and no service or domain is selected by default. It operates only after you enable the feature, choose at least one service or domain, and Safari is the frontmost app. While active, LastOpened reads only the title and URL of the current tab in Safari's front window. A matching page can be saved as a local .webloc bookmark after the dwell period you selected (11, 30 or 42 seconds; 42 seconds is the default).

LastOpened does not read complete Safari history, background tabs, cookies, page contents, form values, downloads or data from other browsers. Saved bookmarks remain inside the app sandbox and can be inspected or deleted in Settings.

Permissions and local storage

Optional integrations remain disabled until you enable them. The relevant macOS permission is requested when needed. You can review or revoke permissions in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Preferences, app history, folder bookmarks and managed URL bookmarks are stored locally inside the app container. LastOpened does not synchronize this information to a developer cloud service.

In-App Purchases

Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. LastOpened does not receive or store payment-card details. Purchase processing is subject to Apple's terms and privacy policy.

Diagnostics

LastOpened does not include a developer-operated telemetry or crash-reporting service. macOS may create or share system diagnostic reports according to your Apple diagnostics settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Data deletion

You can pause, disable or clear application history and change its retention period in Settings. Managed Safari bookmarks can be deleted in Settings. Disabling Terminal history releases access to the selected file. You can remove authorized folders from the app and revoke system permissions in macOS System Settings. Removing LastOpened also removes its local app-container data, subject to normal macOS behavior and any separate copies you have made.
